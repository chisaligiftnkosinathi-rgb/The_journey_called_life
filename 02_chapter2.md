# CHAPTER 2: THE SEED

Before there was an I, there was information.
Not information in the form of words. Not a book. Not a memory. Not a thought.
Something older. Something written without language.
A sequence. A pattern. A possibility carried forward from one generation to another.
DNA.

We often speak about DNA as though it were a blueprint. The metaphor is useful, but it can also mislead us.
A blueprint describes a finished structure. DNA does not simply contain a miniature human waiting to unfold. It contains biological information that participates in an extraordinarily complicated process of development.
The organism develops through an interaction between inherited information and everything that happens around it.

And yet, something astonishing remains.
Long before I had a memory of myself, biological information existed that helped make my existence possible.
The seed came before the gardener.

## The inheritance
Every human being enters the world carrying an inheritance.
Some of it is obvious: physical characteristics and biological tendencies.
But inheritance is larger than DNA.
A child can inherit a surname, a language, a culture, a religion, a way of speaking, a family story, a relationship with money, a fear, a wound, or a dream that belonged to someone before them.

Some inheritances are written in chromosomes. Others are written in memory.
This means that when I ask: **What made me possible?** I cannot look only at my DNA. I have to look backward. Much further backward.

## Before my parents
My existence has a peculiar mathematical quality.
I have two parents. They had parents. Those parents had parents.
Generation after generation opens backward like a branching tree.
If we continue far enough, the number of ancestors implied by the simple arithmetic becomes enormous.

Populations migrate. Families separate. Wars move people. Drought moves people. Love moves people.
And through all of this movement, biological information keeps travelling until, eventually, it arrives here. At me.
I am the latest visible point in an invisible chain. But the chain did not exist merely to produce me.
My ancestors were not necessarily moving toward *me*. They were surviving, adapting, and dying within the conditions of their own worlds.
I am what emerged from an enormous history of events. Not necessarily what that history intended.

## Can something carry information without carrying intention?
DNA clearly carries biological information. But does information itself imply a sender?
Does a pattern imply a purpose? Or can extraordinarily complex patterns arise through natural processes without conscious intention?
Science can investigate how hereditary information is copied and changed. The question of ultimate purpose is different. It takes us beyond what DNA alone can tell us.

## The seed does not know the tree
Consider an acorn. Inside it is information that participates in the development of an oak.
But the acorn does not need to possess a concept of an oak.
Human development is vastly more complicated, but the metaphor gives us something useful.
Perhaps the beginning of an I is not an I that already knows what it will become.
Perhaps it is potential. And potential is not destiny.

The seed matters. The soil matters. The weather matters.
And in humans, something extraordinary happens:
The organism becomes capable of asking about its own development.
The seed becomes capable of asking: **What am I becoming?**

## A history older than memory
My first memory has a beginning. My biological inheritance does not.
There is an enormous asymmetry here.
I am, in one sense, newer than the information participating in my existence.
The I is young. The biological story behind the I is ancient.

## The family becomes evidence
This is where the investigation can become personal without becoming an autobiography.
My own family history contains examples of this larger human process.
There are stories of movement between countries, of work, of farming, of healing traditions, of migration, and of education.
These stories do not tell me exactly who I am. A family history is not a psychological equation.
But the stories reveal something important: **The I does not begin with the I.**

Before I can make choices, other people have already made choices that affect the conditions into which I arrive.
The soil is waiting before the seed arrives.

## The world was already moving
I was born into the late twentieth century. But the twentieth century did not begin with me.
Neither did South Africa. By the time I appeared, the world had already accumulated enormous momentum.
Industrialization had happened. Colonialism had happened. Apartheid had happened. Computers had emerged.
The world I entered was not an empty stage. It was a moving system.
I was born into momentum.

## Inheritance is not destiny
If I discover that something was inherited, I must not immediately conclude that it controls me.
Influence is not the same as absolute determination.
A human being is not simply a seed placed into soil and left there.
At some point, the organism becomes capable of responding, choosing, and even changing the conditions under which its future self will develop.

## What was handed to me?
What was I told about my inheritance?
Which characteristics did people say came from my father? Or my mother?
Was I told that I was stubborn? Curious? Intelligent?
Where did those descriptions come from?
A description can become an expectation. An expectation can become behaviour. Behaviour can become habit. Habit can become identity.
Now the seed is no longer merely biological. The environment has begun writing alongside biology.

## The information problem
Biology can tell us much about how a body develops.
Neuroscience can investigate how brains process information.
But when the organism asks: **What is the meaning of my existence?** we have crossed into another territory.

The seed has become a living organism. The organism has entered a world.
And somewhere along the way, it will begin to distinguish between:
**the world** and **me.**

The seed will become an experiencer.
And that is where the next investigation begins.
